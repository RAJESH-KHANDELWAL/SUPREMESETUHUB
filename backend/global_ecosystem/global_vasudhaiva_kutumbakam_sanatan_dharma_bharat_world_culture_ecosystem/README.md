# GLOBAL VASUDHAIVA KUTUMBAKAM | SANATAN DHARMA | BHARAT | WORLD CULTURE ECOSYSTEM

Canonical global namespace: `backend/global_ecosystem/global_vasudhaiva_kutumbakam_sanatan_dharma_bharat_world_culture_ecosystem/`

A unified global ecosystem for Vasudhaiva Kutumbakam, Sanatan Dharma heritage, Bharat's civilizational and cultural knowledge, and the world's cultures, languages, heritage, arts, traditions, communities, cultural exchange, and preservation.

## Scope

- Vasudhaiva Kutumbakam and shared human values
- Sanatan Dharma heritage, traditions, philosophy, and knowledge
- Bharat's civilization, history, languages, arts, and cultural institutions
- World cultures, languages, heritage, arts, traditions, and communities
- Cultural exchange, education, documentation, and preservation

## Architecture boundaries

- This directory owns the unified global ecosystem namespace and its documentation/index.
- CORE, API, ENGINE, DATABASE, authentication, and runtime implementations remain in their separately owned architecture layers.
- Do not copy or relocate existing implementations into this namespace.
- Namespace presence does not mean a service, integration, provider, or dataset is implemented or verified.

## Scope and governance

Keep historical research, living traditions, cultural documentation, educational resources, community records, and public services clearly classified. Distinguish historical evidence, tradition, and opinion; cite sources and preserve provenance. Represent cultures respectfully and accurately, retaining regional distinctions, language context, community consent, and source attribution rather than flattening distinct traditions into one taxonomy.
