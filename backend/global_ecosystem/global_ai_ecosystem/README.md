# GLOBAL AI ECOSYSTEM

## Mission and geographic scope

The Global AI Ecosystem is the AI-domain namespace for the full Bharat-to-world landscape: India-first and Indic capabilities, regional ecosystems, international providers, multilingual systems, and cross-border interoperability.

It covers the ordinary, foundational, professional, advanced, research-grade, and aspirational/supreme ends of AI capability. "Supreme" is a long-term ambition and taxonomy tier, not a claim of artificial general intelligence, superintelligence, consciousness, or a currently achieved capability.

## Capability maturity spectrum

- Foundational / task-specific AI
- Assistants and conversational AI
- Predictive, analytical, and decision-support AI
- Generative AI across text, code, image, audio, video, and 3D
- Multimodal and multilingual AI
- Reasoning, planning, and tool-using AI
- Agentic and multi-agent systems
- Autonomous systems under bounded, tested authorization
- Advanced research and frontier AI
- Supreme / long-horizon intelligence research

## Domain coverage

- Bharat / India AI: Indic languages, localization, public-interest use cases, education, agriculture, accessibility, and Indian business contexts.
- Global / world AI: international providers, languages, regions, standards, and interoperability.
- Business and economy: entrepreneurship, commerce, customer support, operations, market intelligence, and productivity.
- Education, knowledge, and research: tutoring, translation, discovery, scientific workflows, and knowledge retrieval.
- Digital infrastructure: software, systems, networks, cloud, hosting, servers, and cybersecurity support.
- Web, websites, and applications: design assistance, development, testing, accessibility, and personalization.
- Media and creative work: writing, images, audio, speech, video, animation, design, and 3D.
- People and society: accessibility, language, culture, public services, and responsible social applications.
- Health, wellness, relationships, and intimacy: educational and informational capabilities with privacy, consent, age-appropriate access, and professional-scope safeguards.
- Lawful adult-industry platform governance: age assurance, consent and rights checks, safety moderation, privacy, and compliance for lawful age-restricted services.

## Adult, sexual-health, and intimacy boundaries

The domain catalog may include lawful adult-industry and sexual-wellness contexts. Any future product or integration must be restricted to adults where required and comply with applicable law and platform policies. It must not facilitate sexual exploitation, trafficking, coercion, non-consensual intimate imagery, or any sexual content involving minors or people who appear to be minors. Explicit content generation or distribution is not implied by this catalog entry.

## Architecture ownership and boundaries

This directory is a domain catalog / namespace only.

- API owns public interfaces and transport contracts.
- CORE owns stable domain contracts and shared business rules.
- ENGINE owns orchestration, model routing, tool execution, and runtime workflows.
- DATABASE owns persistence schemas, migrations, and data access.
- Security, identity, credentials, authentication, authorization, audit, and policy enforcement remain in their canonical security/identity layers.
- Provider-specific integrations remain in their canonical provider ecosystems and must be connected through explicit, tested contracts.

Do not duplicate runtime implementations, secrets, provider SDK execution, database logic, or authentication code in this namespace. Do not imply that a catalogued capability is implemented or live unless independently tested and evidenced.

## Status vocabulary

- SCOPE_DEFINED: domain is described in the taxonomy.
- CATALOGED: represented in an existing catalog.
- ROADMAP: planned, not implemented.
- RESEARCH: exploratory work only.
- POLICY_GATED_SCOPE: requires legal, safety, age, consent, and platform-policy review.
- IMPLEMENTED: code exists and passes relevant tests.
- INTEGRATED: connected through a verified contract and integration test.
- PRODUCTION_READY: meets documented operational, security, reliability, and compliance gates.

A directory, name, README, or roadmap entry alone is not evidence of implementation.

## Integration principles

1. India-first localization with global interoperability.
2. Modular providers and model-agnostic contracts.
3. Evidence-based capability and maturity claims.
4. Least privilege, privacy by design, and auditable operations.
5. Human oversight for consequential actions.
6. Safety, legal compliance, licensing, consent, and age-appropriate controls.
7. Reproducible tests and explicit operational readiness criteria.
