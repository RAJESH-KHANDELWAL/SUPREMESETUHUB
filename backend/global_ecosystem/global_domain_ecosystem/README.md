# Global Domain Ecosystem

Master catalog for domains and sectors from **Bharat/India to the global world**, with maturity levels from **Basic/Normal to Supreme**.

## Domain coverage
- Society and public services: government, civic technology, law, justice, public safety and community development
- Business and economy: entrepreneurship, commerce, finance, banking, insurance, accounting, fintech, trade and direct selling
- Technology: software, websites, apps, AI, data, cloud, DevOps, cybersecurity, telecom, blockchain, robotics and quantum computing
- Knowledge and people: education, research, science, careers, skills, culture, languages and accessibility
- Health and life: healthcare, public health, wellness, relationships, family, sexual health and reproductive health
- Adult-only media and services: lawful adult entertainment platforms, adult creator economy, subscriptions, content distribution, age assurance, consent/release management, privacy, payments, moderation and takedowns
- Media and entertainment: publishing, film, television, music, gaming, esports, events and sports
- Industry and infrastructure: agriculture, food, manufacturing, construction, real estate, energy, water, transport, logistics, aerospace, space, resilience, environment and climate
- Places and services: tourism, travel, hospitality, professional/local services, nonprofits and humanitarian services

## Geographic scopes
Bharat/India, Indian states and union territories, South Asia, Central Asia, East Asia, Southeast Asia, Middle East, Africa, Europe, North America, Latin America/Caribbean, Oceania and global cross-border.

## Maturity levels
Basic/Normal, Standard, Professional, Advanced, Expert, Enterprise/Industrial, Research/Frontier and Supreme/Long-horizon.

## Adult-only safeguards
Adult domains are for lawful, consensual adult use only. Systems must plan for robust age assurance, informed consent, performer release records where applicable, privacy, moderation, reporting/takedown, secure payments and jurisdiction-specific legal review. Excluded: sexual content involving minors, non-consensual intimate imagery, coercion, trafficking, sexual extortion and hidden-camera content.

## Architecture boundaries
This package owns taxonomy, classification, regional metadata, domain profiles and scope validation only. Shared runtime layers remain separate:
- API: external interfaces and transport
- CORE: shared business rules and contracts
- ENGINE: execution and orchestration
- DATABASE: persistence and migrations
- SECURITY/IDENTITY: credentials, authentication, authorization and security controls

The included registry is in-memory only. Modules are not deployed production services, legal certification or live integrations.
