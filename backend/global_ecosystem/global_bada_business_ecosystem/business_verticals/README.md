# Global Business Verticals

This package provides a broad Bharat-to-global business taxonomy for discovery
and planning. It spans basic/normal, standard, professional, advanced, expert,
enterprise, research/frontier and supreme long-horizon maturity labels.

## Scope
The catalogue covers agriculture, food, manufacturing, commerce, finance,
education, health, real estate, technology/AI, media, hospitality, logistics,
energy, professional services, network marketing/direct selling, sports,
fashion, cultural enterprise, public services and frontier ventures.

It also identifies two separate lawful adult-only business verticals:
- Adult relationship, intimacy and sexual-wellness businesses.
- Lawful adult-entertainment industry services, including production support,
  licensed distribution, subscriptions, age assurance, moderation and rights.

## Boundaries
These entries are taxonomy and planning metadata. They do not prove that a
provider offers a service, that a partnership exists, or that a live API is
connected. Adult-industry listings require verified adults, freely given and
revocable consent, documented rights, age assurance, privacy controls,
moderation and jurisdiction review. No exploitation, trafficking or
non-consensual intimate imagery is supported.

The package does not itself host, create or distribute adult content. It is not
legal certification and does not replace local legal review.
