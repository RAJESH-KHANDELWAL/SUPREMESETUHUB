# Operating model

## Roles and accountability
Every operational activity should have a named accountable owner, a responsible operator and an escalation contact. Roles may be combined in an early pilot, but accountability must remain explicit.

- Domain owner: scope, priorities, budget and final operational accountability.
- Cultural/community reviewer: context, representation, consent and community feedback.
- Rights and content reviewer: provenance, licensing, attribution and editorial accuracy.
- Provider onboarding lead: identity, capability, terms and periodic re-verification.
- Customer support lead: inquiries, complaints, refunds and escalation.
- Finance owner: invoices, payout reconciliation, fees and financial controls.
- Security/privacy owner: data access, retention, incident response and vendor review.
- Legal/compliance adviser: jurisdiction-specific advice and required approvals.

## Standard operating procedures
1. Intake: record proposal, requester, purpose, location, affected stakeholders and expected value.
2. Screening: check scope, conflicts, safety, legal restrictions and reputational concerns.
3. Due diligence: verify identity, authority, references, rights and claims.
4. Agreement: document scope, deliverables, pricing, IP, data, cancellation and liability.
5. Quality review: inspect content, product or service against a published checklist.
6. Publication: show only approved and current information with appropriate status labels.
7. Delivery: confirm capacity, terms, support and completion.
8. Reconciliation: match orders, invoices, fees, refunds and provider payouts.
9. Feedback: review complaints, corrections and outcome metrics.
10. Renewal or closure: re-verify, renew, pause or archive with an audit trail.

## Recordkeeping
Maintain versioned records for provider verification, consent, rights, agreements, content reviews, incidents, payments and corrections. Restrict access according to role and retention requirements. Do not store credentials or payment secrets in this package.

## Quality standards
- Claims are evidence-backed and dated.
- Listings show whether information is proposed, under review or verified.
- Prices and terms are understandable before commitment.
- Images and content have usage rights.
- Customer communications are respectful and accessible.
- Complaints have an owner and a documented resolution.
- Expired or disputed information is promptly reviewed or hidden.
