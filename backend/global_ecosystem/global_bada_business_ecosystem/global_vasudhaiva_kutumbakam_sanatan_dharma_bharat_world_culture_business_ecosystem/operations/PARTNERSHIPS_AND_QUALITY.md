# Partnerships, onboarding and quality assurance

## Partner categories
Potential categories include artisan collectives, cultural practitioners, educational institutions, museums, archives, publishers, responsible tourism operators, hospitality providers, event organizers, nonprofits, research groups and ethical businesses. Inclusion here does not imply any existing relationship.

## Due diligence checklist
- Confirm legal or community identity and authorized representative.
- Understand ownership, governance and beneficial interests where relevant.
- Verify qualifications, licenses, references and relevant operating history.
- Review complaints, safety practices and public claims.
- Confirm rights to content, images, designs, recordings and cultural material.
- Check conflicts of interest and any required regulatory screening.
- Agree scope, deliverables, fees, commissions, payment schedule and taxes.
- Define brand use, publicity approval, data access and confidentiality.
- Define cancellation, termination, liability, dispute and takedown terms.
- Recheck material facts at an appropriate interval.

## Written agreement essentials
Purpose and scope; parties and authority; deliverables and acceptance; schedule; fees and taxes; payment timing; expenses; intellectual property; consent and attribution; privacy and data handling; confidentiality; safety and insurance where relevant; cancellation and refunds; complaints and dispute process; term and termination; permitted publicity; and governing law.

## Quality assurance checklist
- Accurate description and current contact details.
- Clear evidence for origin, authenticity and credentials.
- Current permission for all media and cultural materials.
- Transparent price, availability process and service conditions.
- Safe, accessible and lawful delivery arrangements.
- No fabricated reviews, awards, affiliations or endorsements.
- Documented customer support and correction process.

## Partner status vocabulary
- **Proposed:** not yet reviewed.
- **Under review:** evidence and due diligence are being assessed.
- **Verified:** specified claims were checked against recorded evidence on a stated date.
- **Published:** approved information is visible to users.
- **Paused:** temporarily unavailable or under investigation.
- **Rejected:** failed defined eligibility or risk requirements.
- **Archived:** no longer active; historical record retained according to policy.

A verified status is limited to the specific claims checked; it is not a guarantee of future performance.
