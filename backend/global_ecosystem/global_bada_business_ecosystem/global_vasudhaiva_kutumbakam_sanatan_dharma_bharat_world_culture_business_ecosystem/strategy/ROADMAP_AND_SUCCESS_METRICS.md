# Roadmap and success metrics

## Stage 0 — Foundation and accountability
- Appoint accountable domain owner and independent reviewer.
- Define jurisdiction, legal structure and decision rights.
- Establish cultural consent, rights, provenance, privacy and complaints policies.
- Create a controlled catalog schema and evidence standards.
- Exit gate: documented owners, policies, risk register and approved pilot scope.

## Stage 1 — Discovery research
- Interview creators, artisans, educators, community representatives and prospective customers.
- Map existing alternatives and unmet needs.
- Test language, accessibility and trust requirements.
- Avoid publishing partner or provider claims before verification.
- Exit gate: evidence-backed problem statement and pilot hypothesis.

## Stage 2 — Small, consent-based pilot
- Select one or two narrow verticals and a limited geography.
- Onboard a small number of willing participants.
- Use written agreements and manual review where software is not ready.
- Track inquiries, fulfillment, payouts, complaints and community feedback.
- Exit gate: safe delivery, transparent economics and participant approval.

## Stage 3 — Controlled expansion
- Expand only where quality, support, rights management and unit economics remain sound.
- Add languages and regions with local review.
- Establish partner audits and periodic listing re-verification.
- Exit gate: repeatable service quality and sustainable operating capacity.

## Stage 4 — Platform enablement
- Define API, CORE, ENGINE, AI, DATABASE, STORAGE, AUTH, PAYMENT and APP requirements with their respective owners.
- Complete threat modeling, privacy impact review, accessibility testing and integration tests.
- Launch only after operational readiness and rollback procedures exist.

## Metrics

### Trust and governance
- Percentage of active listings with current verification.
- Percentage of cultural assets with complete rights/consent records.
- Time to resolve corrections, takedowns and complaints.
- Number and severity of substantiated cultural harm or safety incidents.
- Percentage of partners with current written agreements.

### Economic fairness
- Creator payout amount and on-time payout rate.
- Creator share of transaction value where measurable.
- Median time from delivery to payout.
- Dispute, refund and cancellation rates.
- Participation and retention across small/local providers.

### Customer value
- Qualified inquiry-to-completion rate.
- Learner/visitor satisfaction and repeat use.
- Content accuracy corrections.
- Accessibility issue resolution time.
- Customer support response and resolution times.

### Community and sustainability
- Community-defined benefit measures.
- Local procurement and local livelihood outcomes where verifiable.
- Environmental and site-capacity compliance for tourism activities.
- Community satisfaction and consent renewal.

## Guardrails
Metrics must be disaggregated responsibly and only where lawful and appropriate. Do not collect sensitive identity data merely to create dashboards. A high transaction count is not success if consent, safety, quality or fairness deteriorates.
