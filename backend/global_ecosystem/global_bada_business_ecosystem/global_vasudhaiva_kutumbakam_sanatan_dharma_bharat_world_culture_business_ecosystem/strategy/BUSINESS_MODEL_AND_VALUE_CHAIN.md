# Business model and value chain

## Value proposition by participant

- **Creators and artisans:** discoverability, attribution, fair market access, education and collaboration opportunities.
- **Learners and visitors:** contextual information, credible learning and clearly described experiences.
- **Communities:** consent-based representation, negotiated benefits and the ability to correct or withdraw material.
- **Institutions and partners:** documented project scopes, reliable coordination and measurable outcomes.
- **Responsible businesses:** qualified discovery, transparent onboarding and trustworthy service information.

## Potential operating models

These are options for validation, not active offers:
1. Directory and discovery: free or paid listings with transparent ranking and verification rules.
2. Transaction facilitation: disclosed service fee or commission only where legally permitted and operationally supported.
3. Education: course, workshop or event fees with clear instructor rights and refund terms.
4. B2B services: research, translation, interpretation, content production and event support.
5. Institutional projects: fixed-scope projects, sponsorships or grants with written agreements and reporting.
6. Membership or subscription: optional benefits with clear renewal, cancellation and data-use terms.
7. Product commerce: seller-of-record and fulfillment responsibilities explicitly assigned before launch.

## Value chain
Community/creator engagement → rights and provenance review → product/service design → quality and safety review → discovery and communication → order/booking or project agreement → delivery → payment/reconciliation → feedback and grievance handling → impact review.

Each stage needs a named owner, evidence record and escalation path.

## Unit economics to validate
Track, where applicable:
- Qualified inquiry rate and conversion rate.
- Average order/project value.
- Creator/provider payout and payout timing.
- Gross margin and contribution margin after support, refunds and payment costs.
- Customer acquisition cost and repeat engagement.
- Refund, cancellation, dispute and complaint rates.
- Verification and moderation cost per active listing.
- Community benefit share and creator income distribution.

Do not optimize revenue by weakening consent, safety, cultural integrity or fair compensation.

## Pricing and commercial controls
- Publish all fees, commissions, taxes and payment timelines before commitment.
- State who is the seller, service provider, agent or intermediary.
- Explain refunds, cancellations, delivery, warranty and dispute terms.
- Do not promise earnings, guaranteed sales, grants, investment returns or customer volume.
- Review pricing for affordability and fair creator compensation.
- Keep business records and tax treatment aligned with applicable jurisdictions.

## Validation before scaling
Conduct interviews with representative creators, community groups, learners and buyers; test a narrow pilot; compare willingness to pay with delivery costs; document adverse impacts; and stop or redesign if benefits do not justify the risks.
