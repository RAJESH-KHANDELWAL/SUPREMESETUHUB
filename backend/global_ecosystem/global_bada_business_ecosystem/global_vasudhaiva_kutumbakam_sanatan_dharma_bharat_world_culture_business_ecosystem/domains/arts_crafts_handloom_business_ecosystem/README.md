# Arts, Crafts and Handloom

**Package:** `arts_crafts_handloom_business_ecosystem`

## Scope
Artisan discovery, craft catalogs, handloom, textiles, pottery, metalwork, woodwork, painting, sculpture, design, commissioned work, exhibitions and workshops. Verify maker identity, origin, rights, attribution, fair pricing and fulfillment.

## Lifecycle
1. Define the offering and participants.
2. Identify providers, creators, rights holders and affected communities.
3. Verify claims, permissions, provenance, credentials and legal requirements.
4. Agree scope, pricing, attribution, compensation and cancellation terms.
5. Pilot with safety and quality checks.
6. Publish only approved, current information.
7. Review outcomes, complaints, impact and renewal.

## Boundary
This package contains domain taxonomy and requirements only. It does not establish a live provider, product, service, booking, payment, accreditation or partnership. Technical implementation remains in separately owned architecture layers.
