# Glossary

- **Vasudhaiva Kutumbakam:** A Sanskrit expression commonly rendered as “the world is one family”; use with context and avoid treating it as a substitute for community consent.
- **Sanatan Dharma:** A term used in varied philosophical, religious and cultural contexts; do not assume a single uniform interpretation.
- **Bharat Sanskriti:** The diverse cultural, linguistic, artistic, intellectual and living heritage associated with India.
- **World culture:** The many distinct cultural practices and creative traditions of communities globally.
- **Cultural practitioner:** A person or group actively engaged in a cultural practice, art, tradition or knowledge system.
- **Knowledge holder:** A person or community recognized by relevant community processes as holding particular knowledge.
- **Provenance:** Documented origin, ownership/custody history and context of an item or piece of content.
- **Consent:** Freely given, informed, specific and appropriately recorded permission; scope and duration matter.
- **Benefit sharing:** Agreed distribution of economic or non-economic benefits with contributors and affected communities.
- **Responsible tourism:** Travel and visitor activity designed to reduce harm and create fair local benefits.
- **Verification:** A documented review of a defined claim; verification is limited to the evidence and date recorded.
- **Endorsement:** Explicit approval by an individual or organization; a listing or partnership must not be presented as endorsement without permission.
- **Domain blueprint:** A documented description of scope and intended capabilities, not a deployed software service.
