# GLOBAL YOUTAG WELLNESS ECOSYSTEM

Parent: `GLOBAL NETWORK MARKETING & DIRECT SELLING BUSINESS ECOSYSTEM`

This namespace represents YOUTAG WELLNESS as a separate wellness/product and
direct-selling business domain.

## Scope

- Wellness/product business namespace and navigation.
- Future catalog references must come from the canonical YOUTAG source layer.
- Distributor, referral, enrollment, order and payment workflows belong in
  their owning runtime/API/database layers.

## Verification boundary

This namespace alone does not verify product claims, regulatory approvals,
official API availability, account access, commissions, payments, or live
transactions. No external connection is configured here.
