# GLOBAL AFFILIATE MARKETING BUSINESS ECOSYSTEM

Parent: `GLOBAL NETWORK MARKETING & DIRECT SELLING BUSINESS ECOSYSTEM`

This is one global affiliate-marketing business ecosystem namespace. It groups affiliate, referral, digital-product promotion, lead-generation and partner-marketing platforms.

## Platform directory

- `i_digitalpreneur/` — iDigitalPreneur platform namespace.
- `leadsguru/` — LeadsGuru platform namespace.
- `millionaire_track/` — Millionaire Track platform namespace.

These are three distinct platforms/programs grouped under the affiliate-marketing business category. This directory does not assert that they are one company, share ownership, or have a common operator. Their legal entities, commercial terms, products, commissions, referral rules and account relationships must be verified independently from official sources.

## Architecture boundaries

This directory is a GLOBAL ECOSYSTEM catalog/namespace only. CORE owns canonical platform and business identities; API owns endpoint contracts; ENGINE owns orchestration and workflows; DATABASE owns persistence; authentication, credentials, payouts and provider connections remain in their respective layers.

Do not copy platform credentials, user accounts, commission ledgers, payout records or live integrations into this namespace. Platform-specific data and workflows must remain isolated unless a verified contract establishes a shared relationship.

## Verification boundary

Namespace registration does not imply official API availability, account access, affiliate approval, commission eligibility, payout processing or live connectivity. Verify official documentation, program terms, legal entity and integration access before implementing those capabilities.
