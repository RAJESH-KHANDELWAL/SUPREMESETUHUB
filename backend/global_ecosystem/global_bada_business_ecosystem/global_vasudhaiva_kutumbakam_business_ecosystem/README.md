# GLOBAL VASUDHAIVA KUTUMBAKAM BUSINESS ECOSYSTEM

Canonical path:
`backend/global_ecosystem/global_bada_business_ecosystem/global_vasudhaiva_kutumbakam_business_ecosystem/`

A distinct business-domain package for ethical, sustainable, and community-benefiting ventures connected with Vasudhaiva Kutumbakam, Sanatan Dharma heritage, Bharat's civilizational knowledge and Sanskriti, and respectful cultural exchange across the world.

## Business domains

- Cultural and heritage tourism, interpretation, and visitor services
- Cultural education, language learning, research, and publishing
- Traditional arts, crafts, handloom, artisan and creator marketplaces
- Ethical products, local enterprises, and community-led commerce
- Events, exhibitions, festivals, workshops, and cultural exchange
- Digital archives, media, content, and educational technology
- Hospitality, travel support, and responsible experience design
- Partnerships, grants, sponsorships, and social-impact ventures

## Governance and safeguards

- Respect cultural, regional, linguistic, and community distinctions; do not treat diverse traditions as interchangeable.
- Record provenance, permissions, ownership, attribution, and consent for cultural material and community knowledge.
- Distinguish verified facts, traditional accounts, interpretations, and commercial claims.
- Use transparent pricing, fair artisan compensation, responsible sourcing, and clear partner disclosures.
- Do not imply endorsement by a religious institution, community, government, or cultural authority without documented authorization.

## Architecture boundaries

This package owns business-domain taxonomy, business resource indexes, and domain documentation only. API, CORE, ENGINE, DATABASE, AI, STORAGE, authentication, payments, and runtime integrations remain in their separately owned architecture layers.

Folder presence is an organizational namespace; it does not mean that a business, marketplace, provider integration, payment flow, or service is implemented or operational.
