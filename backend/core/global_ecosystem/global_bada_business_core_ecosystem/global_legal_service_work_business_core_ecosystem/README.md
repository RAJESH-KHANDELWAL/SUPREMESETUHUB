# Global Legal Service Work Business Core Ecosystem

This ecosystem organizes commercial and professional legal-service work delivered by qualified and appropriately authorized providers.

## Scope
- Legal consultation and advisory services
- Advocate and law-firm service operations
- Litigation, dispute resolution, mediation and arbitration support
- Corporate, commercial and contract law services
- Documentation, drafting, review and compliance support
- Intellectual property, tax, labour, property and family-law services
- Legal research, paralegal and case-management support
- Online legal-service platforms and legal technology operations

## Governance
Legal representation, reserved legal practice, filings and regulated advice must be performed only by professionals authorized in the relevant jurisdiction. The ecosystem taxonomy does not confer a licence or create a lawyer-client relationship.

## Separation
The Global Legal Work Core Ecosystem defines the broad legal-work domain. This Bada Business layer covers providers, service delivery, commercial operations and business workflows.
