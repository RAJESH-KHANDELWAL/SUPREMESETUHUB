# Expanded Global MSME, SME, Startup and Entrepreneurship CORE

Canonical, vendor-neutral taxonomy for Bharat and global enterprise ecosystems. Coverage spans the venture lifecycle; ownership and legal structures; governance, risk, finance, accounting, tax readiness and working capital; procurement, supply chain, people, sales, marketing and customer experience; digital commerce and trade; franchise and cooperative models; sector-specific enterprise domains; product, R&D and IP; sustainability, continuity, M&A, valuation, succession and international expansion; public-private partnerships; social enterprise; inclusion; lifelong business education; analytics; automation; open innovation; and venture portfolios.

- Canonical domains are defined here; service offerings are classified separately in Global Bada Business CORE.
- Business Men, Business Women, women-preneur and men-preneur are descriptive participation domains, not access restrictions.
- Education, learning and capacity-building are lifelong and cross-sector.
- Classification does not mean a business is registered, licensed, funded, verified or guaranteed to succeed.
- The backend 2468 master catalogue is referenced read-only and is not modified by this package.
