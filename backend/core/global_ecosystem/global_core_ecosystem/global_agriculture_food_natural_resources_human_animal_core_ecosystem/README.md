# GLOBAL AGRICULTURE, FOOD & NATURAL RESOURCES — HUMAN & ANIMAL CORE ECOSYSTEM

**Ecosystem ID:** `GLOBAL-AGRICULTURE-FOOD-NATURAL-RESOURCES-HUMAN-ANIMAL-CORE-ECOSYSTEM`

## Purpose
A cross-domain, global-level CORE control plane for agriculture, food systems, natural resources, water, human communities, animals, fisheries, climate, research, technology, trade and governance.

## Responsibilities
- Canonical domain taxonomy and stable identifiers.
- Metadata contracts, capability classification and safety policy.
- Read-only discovery, health and dependency metadata.
- Separation of global knowledge/core capabilities from commercial service delivery.

## Layer boundary
This CORE package does not itself provide farming, food, veterinary, human, financial, trade or environmental services. It does not claim live data, regulatory approval, provider connectivity or operational readiness. Those belong to separately implemented resource, service, API and business layers.

## Business layer
Commercial service, work and business capabilities are maintained separately at:
`../global_bada_business_core_ecosystem/global_agriculture_food_natural_resources_human_animal_service_work_business_core_ecosystem/`

The business layer consumes this canonical taxonomy; it must not fork or redefine the domain identifiers.
