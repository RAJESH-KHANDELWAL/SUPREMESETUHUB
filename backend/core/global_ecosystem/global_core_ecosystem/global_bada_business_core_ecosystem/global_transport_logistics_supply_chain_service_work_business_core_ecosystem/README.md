# GLOBAL BADA BUSINESS TRANSPORT LOGISTICS SUPPLY CHAIN SERVICE WORK BUSINESS CORE ECOSYSTEM

**Parent:** `GLOBAL BADA BUSINESS CORE ECOSYSTEM`  
**Canonical transport taxonomy:** `backend/core/global_ecosystem/global_core_ecosystem/global_transport_road_rail_air_mobility_core_ecosystem/`

## Mission
Classify the commercial and workforce capabilities that use road, rail, air and mobility systems. This package is a Bada Business-facing adapter, not a second transport-domain catalogue.

## Capability groups
1. **LOGISTICS:** freight forwarding, warehousing, fulfilment, last-mile, cold chain, customs and reverse logistics.
2. **SUPPLY CHAIN:** sourcing, procurement, suppliers, demand/capacity planning, inventory, order fulfilment, traceability and risk metadata.
3. **SERVICES:** passenger/freight services, fleet management, terminal/station services, airport ground services, maintenance, ticketing and route-planning service classification.
4. **WORK:** transport operator, pilot/crew, rail operations, warehouse, dispatch, fleet and maintenance role classification; skills and workforce planning.
5. **BUSINESS:** carrier, fleet, freight-forwarder, 3PL/4PL, warehouse, mobility operator, marketplace and infrastructure business models.

## Ownership and boundaries
- Global Transport CORE owns Road, Rail, Air and Mobility domain identity and canonical transport capability metadata.
- This Bada Business CORE owns the logistics, supply-chain, services, work and business classification adapter.
- API, Database, Storage and Engine remain separate layers.
- This catalogue does not claim live job listings, providers, bookings, dispatch, transactions or operational connectivity.
