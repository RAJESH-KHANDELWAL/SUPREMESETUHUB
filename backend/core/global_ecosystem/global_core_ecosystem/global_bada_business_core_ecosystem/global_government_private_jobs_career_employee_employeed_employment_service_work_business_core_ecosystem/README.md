# Global Government & Private Jobs, Careers, Employment — Service Work Business Core Ecosystem

This ecosystem contains the operational and commercial layer for government and private-sector employment services worldwide.

## Scope
- Government employment-service delivery, public recruitment operations, and public-sector workforce services
- Private-sector recruitment, staffing, placement, executive search, and employer hiring services
- Job-board and recruitment-platform operations
- Career guidance, assessment, counselling, resume, and interview service businesses
- HR operations, payroll administration, workforce management, and employer branding services
- Internships, apprenticeships, reskilling, outplacement, outsourcing, BPO, and professional workforce services
- Service providers, business models, delivery workflows, contracts, pricing, compliance, and partner networks

## Separation of concerns
- The Global Core ecosystem owns concepts, classifications, public/private employment models, and reference taxonomies.
- This Global Bada Business ecosystem owns service delivery, business operations, providers, customers, revenue models, staffing operations, and commercial workflows.

## Data integrity
Records should identify jurisdiction, public/private classification, service type, provider, source, verification status, and last-verified date where applicable. Government vacancies and private job listings must not be represented as verified unless supported by an authoritative or directly confirmed source.
