# Global Government & Private Jobs, Careers and Employment Service-Work Business Core Ecosystem

A service-business taxonomy for public-sector and private-sector employment operations: recruitment, staffing, placement, candidate services, HR, payroll, compliance support, outsourcing, workforce technology and labour-market intelligence.

## Architecture
- domain_catalog.py: commercial service domains and service lines
- catalog.py: service models, client types and delivery modes
- policy.py: licensing, transparency, privacy and fair-hiring controls
- service.py: service taxonomy search and provider-record validation
- manager.py: stable Python entry point
- manager.php: PHP metadata facade
- test_ecosystem.py: deterministic offline tests

This is a business-service registry, not a promise of jobs or selection. It does not itself grant recruitment or labour-supply licences, process payroll, provide legal certification, or connect to government procurement systems. All licensing, tender and labour rules depend on jurisdiction.
