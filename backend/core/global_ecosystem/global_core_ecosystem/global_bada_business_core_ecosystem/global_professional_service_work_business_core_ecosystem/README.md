# Global Professional Service Work Business Core Ecosystem

This directory covers the commercial and operational side of professional services and professional work.

It is the business/service counterpart to `global_professional_work_core_ecosystem` under `global_core_ecosystem`.

## Scope

- Professional service firms, agencies, and independent service providers
- Consulting, advisory, accounting, legal-adjacent non-reserved support, design, engineering, and specialist services
- Client acquisition, proposals, contracts, delivery, billing, and service operations
- Outsourcing, managed professional services, partnerships, and business development

Regulated activities must be delivered only by appropriately qualified and authorized providers in the relevant jurisdiction. This taxonomy does not grant licenses or professional credentials.

## Files

- `manager.py`: domain manifest and service-record validation.
- `service_work_business_domains.json`: initial service-business taxonomy.
- `tests/`: retained existing test placeholder.
