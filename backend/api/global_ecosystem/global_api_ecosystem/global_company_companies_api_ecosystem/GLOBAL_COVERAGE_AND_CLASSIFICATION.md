# GLOBAL COMPANY / COMPANIES ECOSYSTEM — COVERAGE AND BOUNDARIES

## Canonical record model
- Company: legally constituted entity or formally registered organization.
- Enterprise: operating economic activity, including sole proprietorships and MSMEs that may not be separate incorporated companies.
- Corporate group: parent, holding, subsidiary and affiliated structures.
- Freelancer: independent person/professional; retained only as a legacy compatibility type and should ultimately be owned by a people/professional ecosystem.

Do not collapse legal identity, operating business, brand, product, platform and individual into one record. Use stable IDs and relationship edges (owns, operates, subsidiary_of, brand_of, founded_by) between canonical records.

## Global classification dimensions
Jurisdiction/country, legal form/status, industry, sector, organization size, ownership, operating model, lifecycle stage and verification are independent dimensions. "Normal", "advanced" and "supreme" are not legal forms; if required, model them as optional capability/maturity tiers only.

## Provenance and coverage
The JSON registry is source-aware and read-only. The empty registry means no global company profiles are currently populated. Country-source metadata is a directory, not a live government connection. Verified states require traceable source URLs. Do not claim complete global coverage without country-by-country source coverage, deduplication and freshness controls.

## Adult industry
Adult-industry companies may be classified as a lawful industry segment where relevant, with jurisdiction, minimum-age, licensing/compliance and safety attributes. This does not override local law or validate any particular company.

## Structural reference boundary
Global Bada Business is used only as a package architecture/layout reference. No Bada Business business catalogue or business-specific data is copied into this ecosystem.
