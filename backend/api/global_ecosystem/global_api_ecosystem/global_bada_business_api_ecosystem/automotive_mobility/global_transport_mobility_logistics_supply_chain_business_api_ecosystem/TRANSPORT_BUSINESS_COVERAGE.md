# Transport Business Coverage Map

## Road Transport & Mobility — `road_transport_mobility/`

Cars, buses, trucks, taxis, cabs, ride-hailing, road freight, commercial vehicles, vehicle rental, intercity transport and last-mile road delivery.

## Railways & Metro Transport — `rail_metro_transport/`

Passenger rail, metro rail, suburban rail, rail freight, container rail, intermodal services, terminals, stations and rail infrastructure services.

## Aviation, Airports & Air Cargo — `aviation_airport_transport/`

Airlines, airports, ground handling, cargo handling, air freight, charter cargo, express air services, aviation logistics and airport services.

## Maritime, Shipping & Ports — `maritime_shipping_ports/`

Container shipping, bulk shipping, coastal shipping, inland waterways, freight forwarding, port services, ship operations and marine logistics.

## Freight, Logistics & Supply Chain — `freight_logistics_supply_chain/`

Full truckload, less-than-truckload, freight brokerage, forwarding, procurement logistics, supply-chain consulting, demand planning, control towers and trade logistics.

## Courier, Parcel & Last-Mile Delivery — `courier_parcel_last_mile/`

Domestic courier, international parcels, express delivery, same-day delivery, document delivery, e-commerce fulfilment and last-mile networks.

## Warehousing, Distribution & Cold Chain — `warehousing_distribution_cold_chain/`

Warehouses, distribution centres, fulfilment centres, inventory handling, cold storage, refrigerated transport, pharma logistics, fresh-food logistics and temperature monitoring.

## Fleet & Vehicle Services — `fleet_vehicle_services/`

Fleet operators, fleet leasing, vehicle maintenance, telematics, driver management, dispatch, fleet insurance coordination and fleet optimisation.

## Passenger Transport & Public Transit — `passenger_transport_public_transit/`

Public transport, buses, taxis, ride-hailing, employee transport, school transport, shuttles, accessible mobility and passenger transit services.
