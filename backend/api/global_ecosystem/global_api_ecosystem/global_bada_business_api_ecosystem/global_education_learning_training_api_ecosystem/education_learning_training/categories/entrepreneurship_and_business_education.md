# Entrepreneurship, MSME & Business Education

Stable category ID: `entrepreneurship_and_business_education`.

Covers entrepreneurship fundamentals, startup and business-model education, MSME management, sales skills, bookkeeping literacy, business planning, market research and leadership development.

This category is for structured learning. It is not a substitute for regulated legal, tax, investment, accounting or financial advice, and it does not certify business success.
