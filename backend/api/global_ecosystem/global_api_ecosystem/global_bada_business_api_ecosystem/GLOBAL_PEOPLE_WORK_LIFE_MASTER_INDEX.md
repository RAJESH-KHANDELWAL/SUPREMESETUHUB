# Global People, Work & Life Ecosystem — Master Directory

## Mission
A global discovery architecture covering people, life stages, education, employment, entrepreneurship, households, relationships, health, finance, daily needs, civic services, communities, travel, technology, environment, property, transport and lawful adult-only domains. This complements the business-sector catalog.

- education_learning_api_ecosystem — early learning, schools, colleges, universities, coaching, vocational learning and scholarships
- jobs_careers_employment_api_ecosystem — government/private/international jobs, careers, internships, remote, freelance, contract and gig work
- entrepreneurship_self_employment_api_ecosystem — home businesses, self-employment, MSMEs, startups, franchises and enterprises
- family_household_caregiving_api_ecosystem — parents, children, homemaking, household operations and caregiving

## Shared facets
- Age/life-stage bands: 0–2, 3–5, 6–12, 13–17, 18–24, 25–34, 35–44, 45–59, 60–74, 75–89, 90–100+; apply age-appropriate access controls.
- Gender and inclusion: inclusive self-description; collect only when relevant and lawful.
- Life situations: student, employed, unemployed, self-employed, entrepreneur, homemaker, parent, caregiver, retired, relocating and user-defined situations.
- Work modes: onsite, remote, hybrid, freelance, contract, gig, seasonal, part-time and full-time.
- Organization sizes: individual, household, community, micro, small, medium, large and enterprise.
- Delivery channels: online, offline and hybrid.
- Geography: global, country, state/province, district, city, neighbourhood and service radius.
- Language, accessibility, affordability, eligibility, provider verification and data freshness.

## Architecture and safety rules
1. A person/life situation is not a business sector. Keep person facets distinct from provider and industry taxonomies.
2. Reuse cross-cutting facets rather than duplicating taxonomies for every age, gender, country or channel.
3. Adult-only categories must be excluded from default discovery and require age assurance, informed consent, privacy controls and jurisdiction-specific legal review.
4. Family safeguarding and survivor support must remain separate from adult entertainment and sexual services. No minors, coercion, trafficking, exploitation or non-consensual imagery.
5. Local law must be resolved at the relevant jurisdiction; do not assume rules are identical across countries, states or cities.
6. Every provider integration must declare source, permissions, jurisdiction, freshness, verification status and limitations.

## Implementation status
These packages are taxonomy/documentation scaffolds. They are not a complete live global directory and do not yet provide provider verification, real-time job listings, bookings, payments, age verification, clinical care, legal determinations or live third-party API integrations. Each capability requires separate implementation, tests, security review and compliance work.
