# Adult Body, Fitness & Intimacy Wellness (18+)

**Domain ID:** `adult_body_fitness_wellness`
**Parent ecosystem:** Kamasutra, Intimacy, Relationship, Adult Health & Wellness
**Coverage:** Bharat + Global
**Access classification:** Adults 18+; consent-first
**Status:** Taxonomy scaffold only

## Scope
Adult-only, non-clinical body confidence and wellbeing education relevant to consensual intimacy. This category is not a general gym directory and does not provide medical diagnosis or treatment.

## Subdomains
- adult body confidence and positive body image
- intimacy-related mobility and flexibility education
- pelvic-floor wellness education with qualified clinical referral boundaries
- adult fitness coaching for general wellbeing and intimacy confidence
- consent-first body awareness and non-clinical wellness education

## Classification boundaries
- General gyms, sports training, nutrition, weight management, yoga and ordinary fitness remain in the Healthcare, Wellness & Fitness ecosystem.
- Pelvic-floor diagnosis, sexual dysfunction treatment and other clinical care belong to licensed healthcare providers and the Healthcare, Medical & Life Sciences ecosystem.
- This folder contains taxonomy labels only, not verified providers, live listings, medical advice or legal approval.

## Governance
- Adults 18+ only; consent-first, privacy-preserving discovery.
- No minors, coercion, exploitation, trafficking or non-consensual content.
- Coaches must stay within non-clinical scope and refer clinical concerns to appropriately licensed professionals.
- Do not store sensitive personal or clinical records here.
