# Universal Products Catalog

Catalog physical and digital products, software, subscriptions, plans, licenses, media and service bundles.

Record canonical product ID, name, brand/vendor, category, version/model, supported regions, language, compatibility, license/terms, price/currency validity window, availability, warranty/support, accessibility, safety notices, provenance, lifecycle and verification source.

Include consumer, business, government, educational, industrial, healthcare, creative, infrastructure and lawful adult wellness product categories. Apply age restrictions, product safety rules, privacy and local law where relevant. Do not misclassify a service or platform as a product without an explicit relationship.
