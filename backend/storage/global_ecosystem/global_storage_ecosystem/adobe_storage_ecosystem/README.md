# Adobe Storage Ecosystem — Bharat to Global Scope

This directory contains the Adobe-specific storage integration namespace and its attached scope taxonomy.

The taxonomy covers **Bharat to Global World** and maturity from **Normal to Supreme**. It includes technology, business, education, culture, health and wellness, and lawful adult-only domains. These folders define organization and scope; they do not imply that Adobe APIs, external providers, or content-storage capabilities are connected or operational.

## Folder structure
- `geography/bharat/`: India, states, union territories, districts, cities, towns and villages
- `geography/regional/`: South Asia and other world regions
- `geography/global/`: international and worldwide coverage
- `maturity_levels/`: Normal, Professional, Advanced, Enterprise and Supreme
- `domains/`: technology, business, education, culture, health/wellness and adult-only topics

## Adult-only governance
Adult-only areas are restricted to lawful adult contexts. Require robust age assurance, informed consent, privacy controls, security, reporting/takedown, retention controls and jurisdiction-specific legal review. Prohibit minors, coercion, exploitation and non-consensual intimate imagery. Taxonomy folders do not authorize hosting or distribution.

## Integration status
This is a folder/file taxonomy. Adobe integration remains `NOT_CONFIGURED` until authorized credentials, permission checks, live health checks and end-to-end tests succeed. Never commit credentials or personal data.
