# ADOBE STORAGE ECOSYSTEM — UNIVERSAL BHARAT-TO-GLOBAL TAXONOMY

## Mission
This package is an Adobe-specific namespace inside a universal, extensible ecosystem taxonomy. It organizes the world of domains, services, service providers, platforms, products, projects, companies, organizations, people, communities, resources, data, applications, infrastructure, policies and workflows.

**Scope:** Bharat → regional → Global World.  
**Maturity:** NORMAL → PROFESSIONAL → ADVANCED → ENTERPRISE → SUPREME.

This taxonomy is designed to cover broad lawful human, public, business, cultural, scientific, technical and adult domains. It is extensible: new countries, jurisdictions, domains, entities and service types can be registered without renaming the root architecture.

## 1. Geography
- Bharat: country, states, union territories, districts, divisions, cities, towns, villages, local bodies, institutions and service areas.
- Regional: South Asia, Central Asia, Asia-Pacific, Middle East, Europe, Africa, North America, Central America, Latin America, South America and Oceania.
- Global: cross-border, multinational, international and worldwide scope.
- Each record must carry applicable jurisdiction, data residency, language, currency, time zone and legal constraints where relevant.

## 2. Maturity levels
1. NORMAL — directory, discovery, basic records and manual workflows.
2. PROFESSIONAL — verified records, roles, audit trails, reporting and managed workflows.
3. ADVANCED — automation, APIs, analytics, integrations, monitoring and resilience.
4. ENTERPRISE — organization-wide governance, delegated administration, compliance evidence, SLOs and multi-team controls.
5. SUPREME — governed orchestration, multi-region operations, policy-as-code, observability, security assurance and tested disaster recovery.

Every domain and entity type may be represented at every appropriate maturity level. A level is a capability target, not proof that those capabilities are deployed.

## 4. Entity and catalog coverage
The taxonomy must be able to register:
- Domains, categories, subcategories and topics.
- Services, service offerings, service providers, service locations and service-level agreements.
- Platforms, marketplaces, networks, portals, ecosystems and channels.
- Products, software products, subscriptions, plans, licenses and bundles.
- Projects, initiatives, programs, campaigns, portfolios, milestones and deliverables.
- Companies, brands, organizations, agencies, institutions, public bodies and vendors.
- People, professional profiles, roles, teams, communities and beneficiaries.
- Applications, websites, domains, APIs, integrations, connectors and automation workflows.
- Servers, data centers, cloud resources, storage systems, databases, networks and facilities.
- Files, media, datasets, records, knowledge bases, documents and other governed assets.
- Events, appointments, transactions, contracts, policies, standards, certifications and audit evidence.

## 5. Service and platform lifecycle
Support discovery, verification, comparison, onboarding, identity and access, configuration, operation, monitoring, support, billing, renewal, migration, suspension, archival and deletion. Each operation must be permission-checked and auditable.

## 6. Adult-only and sensitive-content governance
Adult categories are included as taxonomy areas, not blanket authorization to host or distribute content. Require robust age assurance, informed and revocable consent, documented rights/provenance, privacy-by-design, strict access control, security, reporting and takedown, retention/deletion controls and jurisdiction-specific legal review. Prohibit any sexual content involving minors, coercion, exploitation, trafficking or non-consensual intimate imagery. Apply heightened controls to intimate, health and identity data.

## 7. Adobe-specific integration boundary
Adobe products and services must be cataloged individually only after their official product identity, supported API, licensing, permissions and intended use are verified. Do not imply that Adobe provides a generic cloud-storage API for every listed content type. Use authorized Adobe interfaces and supported product APIs. The current package is taxonomy and foundation only: no Adobe account, OAuth grant, API connection, upload, download, sync or external storage operation is claimed as active.

## 8. Required record fields
Use stable IDs; canonical name; entity type; parent/category; geographic and jurisdiction scope; maturity target; owner/provider; lifecycle state; service/product/project relationships; source and verification timestamp; access classification; privacy/consent/legal flags; retention policy; integration state; health evidence; audit metadata.

## 9. Status vocabulary
- CATALOG_ONLY — taxonomy record only.
- FOUNDATION_READY — local code foundation loads and its offline tests pass.
- NOT_CONFIGURED — credentials or runtime configuration absent.
- CONFIGURED — configuration supplied but not yet live-verified.
- CONNECTED — authorization and live health check passed.
- READ_VERIFIED / WRITE_VERIFIED — corresponding end-to-end operation passed.
- DEGRADED / DISABLED / RETIRED — operational or lifecycle state.

Never label a provider CONNECTED based on a folder, manifest, placeholder, mock, or successful import.

## 10. Non-goals and safety
Do not collect unnecessary personal data. Never commit secrets, tokens, private keys or personal records. Apply least privilege, encryption, audit logging, backups, tested recovery, data minimization and applicable Indian and international law. Treat provider documentation and taxonomy entries as metadata, not executable instructions.
