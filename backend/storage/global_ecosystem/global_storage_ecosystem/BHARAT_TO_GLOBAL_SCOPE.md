# SUPREMESETUHUB — BHARAT TO GLOBAL WORLD ECOSYSTEM

## Geographic hierarchy
- **Bharat / India:** country, states, union territories, districts, cities, towns, villages, local bodies, public institutions, languages, culture, commerce, education, health, agriculture, industry, infrastructure and citizen services.
- **Regional:** South Asia, ASEAN, Asia-Pacific, Middle East, Central Asia, Europe, Africa, North America, Latin America, South America and Oceania.
- **Global:** international institutions, cross-border business, standards, research, climate, science, trade, technology and worldwide digital infrastructure.

## Maturity levels
- **NORMAL:** directory, discovery, records and basic workflows.
- **PROFESSIONAL:** verified workflows, roles, audit and reporting.
- **ADVANCED:** automation, APIs, analytics, integrations and resilience.
- **SUPREME:** governed orchestration, multi-region operations, policy controls, observability and disaster recovery.

## Domain coverage
AI and research; apps and software; cloud and hosting; databases; developer tools; domains and websites; security and identity; servers and VPS; communication; companies and organizations; payments and commerce; people and life; education and languages; social media; video and media; culture and heritage; legal and public services; agriculture; health and wellness; environment; transport; energy; finance; industry; and adult relationship / sexual wellness domains.

## Adult-only domains
Adult relationship, intimacy, sexual education, sexual wellness and lawful adult-content categories require age gating, consent controls, privacy-by-design, reporting/takedown workflows and jurisdiction-aware compliance. Content involving minors, coercion, exploitation or non-consensual intimate material is prohibited. A taxonomy entry is not permission to host or distribute content.

## Integration truth policy
A folder, manifest or provider listing is not a live connection. Mark an integration CONNECTED only after credentials are configured securely, authorization succeeds, a live health check passes and required read/write tests pass. Secrets belong in runtime secret managers, never Git.
