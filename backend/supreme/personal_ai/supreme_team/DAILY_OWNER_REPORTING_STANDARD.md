# DAILY OWNER REPORTING STANDARD

The CMD must deliver one consolidated daily report to the Owner. Department leads submit their own evidence-backed reports to CMD before the agreed daily cutoff.

## Required report sections

1. Executive summary: overall status, major outcomes, decisions needed.
2. Business and sales: leads, prospects, clients, orders, conversions, follow-ups.
3. Finance: opening/closing position where available, income, expenses, receivables, payables, budget variance. Never fabricate figures.
4. Projects and delivery: task, accountable person, planned date, actual status, evidence, blockers.
5. Marketing: channel activity, campaign results, content published, leads attributed.
6. People and HR: attendance where lawfully collected, staffing, workload, hiring pipeline, training, workplace concerns. Restrict personal data to authorized recipients.
7. Technology and GitHub: repository, branch, PR, commit, checks, deployments, incidents, security findings.
8. Office and operations: facilities, inventory, customer service, operational issues.
9. Risks and incidents: severity, impact, containment, owner, next update.
10. Owner decisions required: exact decision, options, recommendation, deadline, consequences of delay.
11. Tomorrow's plan: priorities, responsible role, due time, dependencies.

## Transparent movement and change log

For every task, system, file, folder, project, or workflow movement, show:

- What it was called and where it was located before.
- What it is called and where it is located now.
- Who requested and who performed the move/change.
- Why it was necessary.
- When it happened, with timezone.
- Exact before/after values or paths.
- Approval reference, if approval was required.
- Commit/PR/ticket/evidence reference.
- Verification result and rollback reference.

If nothing moved or changed, explicitly state: "No verified location or structure changes today."

## Accuracy rules

Label each item VERIFIED, ESTIMATED, BLOCKED, PROPOSED, or NOT VERIFIED. Link evidence where possible. Separate completed work from proposed work. Include missed deadlines and failures; do not hide or rewrite them. Never invent employee activity, income, client results, or system changes.
