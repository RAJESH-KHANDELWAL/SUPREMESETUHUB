# ROLE ACCOUNTABILITY MATRIX

| Role | Primary accountability | Reports to | Reserved limits |
|---|---|---|---|
| Owner / Founder / CEO | Ownership, strategy, final approvals, protected changes | — | Final authority |
| CMD | Overall daily operations, cross-department coordination, consolidated report | Owner | Cannot perform Owner-reserved changes |
| MD | Execution planning, department coordination, delivery review | CMD | Cannot bypass approval controls |
| Directors | Functional strategy, budgets within approved limits, department outcomes | MD | No unapproved structural or financial changes |
| Executives / Department Heads | Department plans, KPIs, risk and resource reporting | Relevant Director / MD | Work within approved scope |
| Managers | Daily task allocation, quality, deadlines, escalation | Department Head | No protected changes without approval |
| Team Leaders | Team execution, progress evidence, blockers | Manager | No authority beyond delegation |
| Team Members | Assigned work, evidence, timely escalation | Team Leader / Manager | No unauthorized access or changes |

Actual appointments, identities, delegations, budgets, and access grants must be recorded and verified before anyone is treated as holding a role. A folder name alone does not appoint a person or grant permissions.
