# SUPREME TEAM GOVERNANCE CHARTER

## 1. Ownership and final authority

The Owner, DR RAJESH KHANDELWAL IBC, retains ultimate ownership and final authority over the business and its systems. CMD is the highest delegated operational authority, reporting directly to the Owner.

The CMD may coordinate, assign, supervise, request information, monitor execution, and consolidate reports. CMD does not become the owner and cannot override Owner-reserved decisions.

## 2. Owner approval is mandatory

No team member, CMD, MD, director, executive, manager, automation, or AI agent may independently perform any of these actions:

- Rename, move, or delete a repository file, folder, branch, repository, database object, business identity, domain, or system resource.
- Change, replace, overwrite, or materially alter existing code, configuration, policies, business records, ownership, access controls, or operational structure.
- Merge a pull request, deploy a production change, migrate data, rotate or revoke credentials, or change billing/payment destinations.
- Hire, terminate, appoint, remove, or change the authority of a senior role-holder.
- Make a financial commitment or transfer funds outside a separately approved budget and delegation.

Such actions must be recorded as a proposal and presented to the Owner with the reason, impact, exact before/after state, risk, rollback plan, and affected resources. Execution requires explicit, recorded Owner approval for that specific action. Silence is not approval.

## 3. Permitted delegated operations

Within an Owner-approved scope and budget, teams may perform routine non-destructive work: research, planning, drafting, client follow-ups, task coordination, monitoring, preparing reports, creating proposed work, and maintaining task status.

Where a task requires a protected change, the team prepares a reviewable proposal or pull request and waits for approval. It must not bypass branch protection or approval controls.

## 4. Reporting line

Owner -> CMD -> MD -> Directors -> Executives / Department Heads -> Managers / Team Leaders -> Team Members.

The CMD coordinates the whole operation. MD coordinates execution across departments. Each department head remains accountable for their own workstream. Work must be delegated; no single person is expected to personally perform every function.

## 5. Evidence and accountability

Every material task must have an owner, responsible role, status, due date, evidence, and next action. Reports must distinguish verified facts, estimates, risks, blockers, and proposals. Never report an action as completed without evidence.

## 6. Workplace standards

The business must maintain a lawful, safe, respectful, non-discriminatory workplace. Personal clothing and identity must be treated respectfully subject to lawful role-specific safety requirements. No person may be pressured into nudity, sexual conduct, relationships, or disclosure of private information. Sexual harassment, coercion, retaliation, and exploitation are prohibited. Personal consensual life is private and is not a workplace performance activity.
