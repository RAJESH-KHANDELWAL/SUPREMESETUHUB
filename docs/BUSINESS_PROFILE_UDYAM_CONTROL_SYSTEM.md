## Gate 1 — Establish the legal business record first

1. Locate the current Udyam certificate and verify the enterprise name, Udyam Registration Number, constitution, and address directly on the official portal.
2. Do not create a second Udyam registration just to separate brand names. Official guidance says one enterprise must not file more than one Udyam registration; multiple activities can be specified/added in one registration.
3. Determine whether each proposed activity is part of the same enterprise or a genuinely separate legal enterprise. Confirm the appropriate treatment from official guidance or a qualified professional before filing changes.
4. Record only status and masked references in this repository; keep certificate files and sensitive identifiers private.

**Official portal:** https://udyamregistration.gov.in/  
**Official Udyam guidance:** https://www.udyamregistration.gov.in/Important.aspx

## Gate 2 — Google Business Profile eligibility

Before creating or changing any profile, confirm for each business:

- It is a real, distinct, eligible business—not merely another service label for the same business.
- Its real-world business name is used consistently.
- If showing a customer-facing address, the location is real and staffed as required, customers can visit during stated hours, and permanent fixed signage shows the business name.
- If the business travels to customers and has no eligible storefront, assess a service-area profile and hide the residential address where required.
- A virtual office, rented address, or another area's address must not be entered solely to bypass validation.
- If two genuinely distinct eligible businesses share an address, keep evidence of their distinct operations and visible signage; Google may ask for proof.

**Official Google guidelines:** https://support.google.com/business/answer/3038177?hl=en-IN  
**Google guidance for duplicate profiles:** https://support.google.com/business/answer/12756178?hl=en-GB
