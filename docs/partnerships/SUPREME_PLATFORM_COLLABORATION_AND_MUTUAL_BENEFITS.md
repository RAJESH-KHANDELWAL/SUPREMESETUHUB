## Non-negotiable principle: permission + mutual benefit

Contacting us is an invitation to discuss—not acceptance of a deal. Before any work, promotion, data sharing, platform connection, customer hand-off, API access, or order processing starts, both parties must explicitly approve the scope and the written terms.

No party may assume free work, guaranteed sales, guaranteed income, exclusivity, ownership transfer, access to customer data, or a commission that has not been agreed in writing.

## Flexible benefit models

Each proposal can choose one model or a clearly documented combination:

1. **Percentage-based:** an agreed percentage of a defined, attributable, eligible revenue base.
2. **Order-based:** a fixed benefit per eligible, completed and non-refunded order.
3. **Tiered:** different rates or fixed benefits based on mutually agreed order/revenue thresholds.
4. **Referral/lead-based:** a fee or benefit for qualified leads or converted referrals, only when the qualifying event and attribution rules are written down.
5. **Fixed-fee/project-based:** agreed fee for a defined deliverable or milestone.
6. **Reciprocal/in-kind:** agreed services, promotion, credits, tools, distribution, or other measurable non-cash value.
7. **Hybrid/custom:** a combination tailored to the specific collaboration.

**There is no universal default percentage or automatic entitlement.** The parties must set the exact rate/amount, eligible products or services, currency, calculation base, attribution window, taxes/fees treatment, payout trigger, payout schedule, refunds/chargebacks handling, caps (if any), reporting, and termination treatment before launch. A blank or unapproved term means the collaboration is not approved to go live.

## Suggested order-benefit calculation

For an order-based or percentage model, define the base explicitly. A suggested default for discussion—not an automatic rule—is eligible net item revenue actually collected, excluding cancelled/refunded amounts and separately stated shipping/tax, unless the signed agreement says otherwise.

- Percentage benefit = agreed rate × agreed eligible base.
- Fixed order benefit = agreed amount × qualifying completed orders.
- Tiered benefit = the agreed rate/amount for the tier reached under the written rules.

No benefit should be marked payable until the qualifying event has been verified and the applicable refund/chargeback window and agreement rules have been applied.

## Consent and permissions

Before connection or launch, record the permissions separately:
- permission to contact and discuss the proposal;
- permission to publish the partner's name/logo or announce the collaboration;
- permission for each API, account, data scope, webhook, or platform connection;
- permission to share leads/customer information, with a lawful basis and appropriate notice/consent;
- approval of commercial terms and the person authorized to approve them.

Use least-privilege provider access, official provider APIs, secure server-side secrets, and auditable consent/approval records. Never put passwords, API keys, OAuth secrets, payment credentials, or private customer data in public repositories or public forms. Either party may decline a proposed permission. Revocation and offboarding must be documented; legal/financial records that must be retained can be handled under applicable requirements.

## Ownership and separation

Each provider retains ownership of its platform, accounts, customer relationships, products, prices, and credentials unless a signed agreement explicitly states otherwise. SUPREMESETUHUB may act as a central discovery/routing layer; it must not silently merge provider ownership or move products, orders, customers, or commissions between ecosystems. Each platform's orders and benefits must remain attributable to the correct provider and agreement.

## Safe collaboration workflow

1. Receive a proposal and the partner's preferred contact method.
2. Confirm identity, company/platform, official domain, business purpose and authorized representative.
3. Define deliverables, responsibilities, dates, eligible products/services and success criteria.
4. Choose a benefit model and complete every relevant commercial term.
5. Record explicit approvals and sign/accept the written agreement.
6. Configure only the approved integrations and minimum required permissions.
7. Test attribution, order status, refunds, reports and benefit calculations with test data.
8. Launch only after both sides approve the test results.
9. Reconcile eligible events and pay/settle under the written schedule.
10. Support disputes, termination, access revocation and data deletion/retention according to the agreement and applicable law.

Never pay a registration, recruitment, linking, tax-release or payment-release fee just to be considered for a collaboration. Verify the counterparty and terms independently.
